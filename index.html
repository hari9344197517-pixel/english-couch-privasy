Privacy Policy

How the English Coach app handles information when you use it.

Effective date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to English Coach. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the English Coach mobile application. We aim to respect your privacy and be transparent about our practices.

2. App features

English Coach provides English-learning activities, including:

  • English grammar and knowledge quizzes
  • Daily English learning challenges
  • English paragraph-reading exercises
  • Quiz scores and learning progress

3. Information and permissions

The app's learning activities do not require you to submit your name, phone number, home address, voice recordings, or microphone audio. The app does not provide AI voice conversations, speech-to-text, or AI chat features.

Quiz answers, scores, and learning progress may be stored locally on your device to support the learning experience. If the app does not transmit this information to the developer or a third party, it remains on your device.

4. Personal information

We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or address in order to complete the learning exercises.

5. Third-party services

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the app. Learning information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. The exact result may depend on your device and its backup settings.

7. Children's privacy

English Coach is an educational app. The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
